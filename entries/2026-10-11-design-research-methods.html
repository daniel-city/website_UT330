Reflection on Design Research Methods

October 11, 2026 · Week 7

On October 7th, we asked AI to play a specific role of someone relating to a coffee shop, to get their opinions on if a fictional coffee shop should close later in the day.

Previous Coursework Connections

This reminds me of two previous exercises: in UT101, we were asked to debate AI about topics relating to urban technology. In UT210, we interviewed different people about a specific topic. This exercise combined the two exercises that I’ve engaged in previously, which was particularly interesting.

Something I Learned

I think this exercise was useful in learning how to phrase questions to get the most useful information. For instance, something that was mentioned in class was to not reveal too much information about the goal of conducting the interview (in this case, to decide if the coffee shop should be open later) to get an unbiased interview about the coffee shop.

Overall Reflection

Overall, I enjoyed this exercise because it was a unique way to engage in interviewing skills, and learning what questions to ask, and how. It also encouraged me to think about the various perspectives we could interview about the coffee shop, and how various perspectives play an important role in deciding if a coffee shop should be open later.
