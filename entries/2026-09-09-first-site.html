Reflection on the first two weeks of UT330

September 11, 2026 · Week 2

During my first week of UT330, I learned about interaction design. Specifically, we focused on hierarchy of information, and how information can be displayed in the most intuitive and easy-to-read formatting.

Design On Paper

Something in particular that I enjoyed doing was learning about design through working on paper. It was a different way of thinking about website design that helped encourage me to think about designing websites in a more creative way. When I’ve designed websites previously, I always felt a bit restricted with the tools available, and it would typically take a long time to make different iterations or versions of my design.

On paper, it was significantly easier to make changes in design ideas, and design creative website blocks. Something in particular it helped me with was focusing more on the information hierarchy and the overall layout of information, rather than colours and fonts. When I design websites online, I typically find myself spending significant amounts of time choosing fonts and colours. On paper, that’s not the priority, allowing myself to focus on design hierarchy.

For instance, when I was designing the website for the hospital on paper for the exercise, I had an idea to draw a visual diagram showing where to park, where to enter the hospital, and where to take the elevator up to the second floor. I showed this information with an arrow that flowed throughout the entire design. This is something that I would not have been able to easily do on a website builder (if I was using a website builder, I likely would have just written the instructions with words, rather than drawing it out).

MySpace and Riverton

During our second week, I like how we brought what we learned from drawing websites by hand into starting to work digitally. It helped because I was able to bring my out-of-the-box thinking skills from the first week into designing creative myspace accounts and a library website for (fictional) Riverton. I especially had a lot of fun making a fictional myspace account! It felt fun designing a social media profile through HTML, as it was a unique way to create a social media profile where I had a lot of creative control with customization.
