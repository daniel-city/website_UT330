Reflection on Product Team Sprint

October 11, 2026 · Week 7

During the week of September 28, my team and I worked on a Product Team Sprint to design an app for a fictional city: Riverton. We designed a fictional Riverton 311 app, where people can report broken streetlights to be fixed.

What Was Different This Time

Something that was different about this design sprint from previous Figma projects I’ve worked on is that this time, we were provided with a specific set of graphics, colour themes, and elements for our design. For previous Figma projects, I’ve generally designed without a set of elements to use.

What I learned

I think something that this helped me with was focusing more on functionality rather than design. In previous weeks, when we did a lot of drawing designs on hand, I focused more on creative design and focused more on creating visually appealing designs. However, during this week, since we were given elements to work with, I focused a lot more on creating a cohesive design language that was easy to understand.

Something that I noticed I focused a lot more on was if everything was easy to understand, including the graphics I chose, the layout I chose, and if someone would understand what button to click for what. Since this was a group project, something else that was important to keep in mind was to create a design language that was consistent between each of our frames.

Overall Thoughts

Overall, I think this design sprint focused on something different from what I’m used to with Figma. This week, I focused a lot more on creating a consistent design language that was easy to understand.
